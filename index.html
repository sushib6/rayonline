How We Prepare "Bent" Unprocessed Tobacco

Unprocessed tobacco must be properly conditioned and "bent" during barn curing so leaves don't crumble during transport to manufacturing facilities.

1

Hand Harvest

Ripe tobacco leaves are hand-picked at peak maturity from farm stalks.

2

Barn Curing

Leaves are hung in temperature and humidity-controlled curing barns for 4-8 weeks.

3

Bending & Tying

Flexible, moisture-conditioned leaves are carefully bent and bundled without breaking stems.

4

Ready For Processing

Baled and ready for direct shipment to commercial processing facilities globally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing raw bent tobacco from RayOnline.

What does "bent" tobacco mean?

"Bent" tobacco refers to raw, barn-cured tobacco leaves that have been conditioned with optimal natural moisture so they can be bent, folded, or pressed into farm bales without shattering. This keeps the leaf intact until it reaches the processing plant.

Can I inspect the tobacco barn in person?

Yes! Simply use our "Book Inspection" section above to select a date and time slot. Our farm managers will prepare sample bales for your inspection team at the RayOnline facility.

What is the minimum bulk order quantity?

Our standard minimum order is 3 to 5 bales (approx. 300kg - 500kg). For factory trial batches, we also offer custom 5kg - 25kg sample packs upon request.
